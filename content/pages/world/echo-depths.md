## How runs work

Each run costs 1 [[currency:echo-keys|Echo Key]]. You get 1 key every 3 hours.

## Rewards

- [[currency:echo-marks]] (common) and [[currency:echo-cores]] (rarer), spent at the [[place:archivist]] and [[place:shard-fuser]].
- [[potion:echo-elixir]]: +25% luck for 30 minutes. Only obtainable here.
- **Zone shards:** every finished run gives 1 of 15 shards for that zone. At 15, unlock the zone's special card at the [[place:shard-fuser]].

:::tip
[[potion:incense]] gives +50% Echo Marks.
:::
