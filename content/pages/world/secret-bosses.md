There are **8 secret bosses**: 2 in the [[world:lobby]] and 1 in each of the 6 worlds. Their locations are hidden, so each has a route guide below.

## How they work

- Their cards have **higher base stats** than cards of equal rarity.
- Difficulties: **Normal, Hard, Extreme, Nightmare**.
- Harder difficulties give a better chance to unlock rolling the boss's card.

{{progress label="Pity: Nightmare clears for a guaranteed unlock" value=140 max=140 suffix="clears" tier="6" note="140 Nightmare clears guarantees the card unlock."}}

:::warning
Secret bosses get stronger every time you prestige, like every other NPC enemy. See [[place:prestige-tower]].
:::

:::tip
The [[gamepass:auto-boss]] pass can fight the bosses and difficulties you pick from anywhere.
:::
