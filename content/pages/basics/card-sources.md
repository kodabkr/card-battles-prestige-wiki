## Source matters

Two cards with the same odds can have very different stats. At the same rarity, these cards have **higher base stats** than general-pool cards:

- [[Card Packs|Pack cards]]
- [[Weathers|Weather-exclusive cards]]
- Boss cards
- [[Secret Bosses|Secret boss cards]]

For example, a 1 in 100M [[weather:cataclysm]] card can be several times stronger than a general 1 in 100M card.

## Where each kind of card drops

| Source | How to roll it |
|---|---|
| General pool | Roll with no pack equipped. |
| Pack | Equip the pack. See [[Card Packs]]. |
| Weather | Roll while that weather is active. See [[Weathers]]. |
| Boss | Use a [[potion:boss-potion]] to roll cards of bosses you have fought. |
| Secret boss | Beat the boss to unlock rolling its card. See [[Secret Bosses]]. |
| Raid enemies | Unlock them in the [[place:kaiju-raid-shop]] or [[place:shadow-market]]. |
| Echo Depths zones | Fuse 15 zone shards at the [[place:shard-fuser]]. |

:::note
On top of the source, two more things raise a card's stats: its [[Borders|border]] and its pack level at the [[place:pack-upgrader]].
:::
