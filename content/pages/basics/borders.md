A **border** is a special version of a card, such as **Shiny**, **Galactic** or **Shiny Galactic**. A border raises the card's rarity, which raises its DMG and HP.

## Borders and scrap

Each border counts as its own card when you sacrifice duplicates for [[currency:scrap]]. You keep 4 copies of each version:

- 5 Shiny Galactic copies → 1 to sacrifice.
- 4 plain Galactic copies → nothing to sacrifice.
