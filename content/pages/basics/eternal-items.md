When you prestige, most things reset. **Eternal** items and potions are kept.

:::eternal
Look for the **Eternal** badge on any item. All [[Charms & Relics|relics]] are eternal, and so is [[charm:tinkers-charm]].
:::

:::resets
Prestiging resets:

- All **non-eternal potions**, including any time left on them
- Your [[place:infinite]] progress
- [[gamepass:double-roll|Double Roll]] earned from the quest line (the game pass version stays)
- Cards: TODO(exactly what happens to your cards)
:::
