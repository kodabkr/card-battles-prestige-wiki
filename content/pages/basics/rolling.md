## Rolling

You roll to get cards. Each card has a rarity shown as odds: **1 in N**. A 1 in 1,000 card shows up about once every 1,000 rolls before any luck is applied.

With no [[Card Packs|card pack]] equipped you roll from the **general pool**. Pack-exclusive cards only drop while that pack is equipped, and weather-exclusive cards only during that [[Weathers|weather]].

## Your rolling stats

|---|---|

| **Luck (percent)** | Shown as +N%. Raises your chance of rarer cards. |
| **Roll speed** | How fast you roll. |

## Borders

Some cards roll with a **border** such as Shiny or Galactic. A border raises the card's rarity, which raises its DMG and HP. See [[Borders]].
