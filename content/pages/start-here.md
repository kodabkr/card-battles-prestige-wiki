Card Battles: Prestige is about **rolling cards**, building a team, and pushing ever higher in the [[place:prestige-tower]]. This path takes you through the basics in order. Each step links to a full page.

## 1. Learn how rolling works

You roll for cards. Each card's rarity is shown as odds, like **1 in 1,000**. Your **luck** makes rare cards more likely, and **roll speed** makes you roll faster. Read [[Rolling & Luck]].

## 2. Know where good cards come from

Two cards with the same odds are not equal. Pack, weather, boss and secret boss cards have higher base stats than general-pool cards. Read [[Card Sources]].

## 3. Use potions

Potions boost luck, roll speed and battle speed for a while. Read [[Potions]].

## 4. Fight through the worlds

Each world has bosses, and beating them unlocks their cards. There are also 8 hidden [[Secret Bosses]]. Read [[Worlds]].

## 5. Farm Infinite

[[place:infinite]] is an endless battle mode that drops cards and the materials you need for crafting.

## 6. Craft charms, buy relics

Charms and relics are big passive boosts. Read [[Charms & Relics]].

## 7. Climb the Prestige Tower, then prestige

Higher floors give more prestige luck. Before you prestige, learn what you keep and what you lose.

:::resets
Prestiging resets your non-eternal potions, your Infinite progress and more. Read [[Eternal Items]] first.
:::

## 8. Raids and side areas

When you are stronger, run the [[place:kaiju-raid]] and [[place:shadow-garden-raid]] for relics and potions, and try [[place:echo-depths]] from [[place:the-hall]].
