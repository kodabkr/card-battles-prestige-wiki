## How it works

Infinite is an endless series of 4v4 battles. It drops cards and items, including crafting materials such as [[material:enchanted-core]] and [[material:spirit-dust]].

## Getting more drops

- **[[stat:item-luck]]** raises how many items you get each time Infinite drops items.
- **[[gamepass:bonus-drops]]** gives a 50% chance to double the items dropped.
- **[[stat:tower-speed]]** speeds up battles inside Infinite.

:::resets
Your Infinite progress resets when you prestige.
:::
