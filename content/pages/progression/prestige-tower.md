## How it works

The Prestige Tower is a climb of **4v4 battles**, like [[place:infinite]]. Each floor you clear moves you up.

Your **prestige luck** grows at milestones **every 100 floors**.

:::tip Your route does not matter
Prestige luck depends only on the floor you are on now. Going 1 → 250 → 1,000 gives the same luck as going 1 → 1,000.
:::

## What prestiging resets

:::resets
- All non-eternal potions, including any time left on them
- Your [[place:infinite]] progress
- [[gamepass:double-roll|Double Roll]] earned from the quest line
- Cards: TODO(exactly what resets)
:::

:::eternal
Eternal items and potions are kept, and so are all relics. See [[Eternal Items]].
:::

## Enemy scaling

Every NPC enemy, **secret bosses included**, gets stronger each time you prestige.

## Rewards for climbing

Reaching certain floors also unlocks rewards in the [[place:victory-road]], such as a second relic slot at floor 3,500.
