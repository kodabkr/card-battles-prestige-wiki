Roles describe what a card's ability mainly does. They are **the wiki's own labels**, worked out from each card's ability text, so you can find cards by playstyle. The game does not show them.

Each card has one **main role** (listed first on its profile) and up to three more. In the [[Card Database]] you can pick one or more roles to find, for example, a battle card that mainly supports the team.
