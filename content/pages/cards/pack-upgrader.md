## How it works

The Pack Upgrader is in the [[place:science-lab]]. Rolling on a pack earns XP toward levelling it, and each level **multiplies that pack's card stats**.

{{progress label="Example: Ascended Pack at level 5" value=5 max=8 suffix="levels" tier="4" note="At level 5 of 8, Ascended cards get 3.25x stats."}}

:::tip
A [[potion:pack-xp-tonic]] gives more Pack Upgrader XP while you roll.
:::
