With no pack equipped you roll from the **general pool**. Equip a pack to roll its exclusive cards. Pack cards have higher base stats than general-pool cards of the same rarity.

:::tip
The [[gamepass:double-pack-equip]] pass lets you roll from 2 packs at once.
:::

## Levelling packs

Rolling on a pack earns XP toward levelling it at the [[place:pack-upgrader]]. Each level multiplies that pack's card stats.
