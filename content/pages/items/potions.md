There are three main potion types, each in **tiers 1–6**:

- **Luck.** Low tiers give flat luck. From tier 3 they also give percent luck.
- **Roll speed.** Multiplies how fast you roll.
- **Battle speed.** Multiplies how fast battles play out.

Some potions boost the same stats outside the tier system: TODO(which ones). There are also special potions with unique effects.

:::tip
The [[gamepass:longer-potions]] pass makes every potion last 50% longer.
:::
