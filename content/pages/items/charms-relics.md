## Charms

There are **13 charms**. You craft them from materials in the crafting menu. Some materials only drop from specific bosses.

- **Slots:** 1 by default, 2 with the [[gamepass:extra-charm-slot]] pass.
- **On prestige:** most charms are **not** eternal. [[charm:tinkers-charm]] is.

## Relics

Relics work like charms but have their own inventory.

:::eternal All relics are eternal
You keep every relic when you prestige.
:::

## Item luck

Some relics give **item luck**. It raises how many items you get when [[place:infinite]] drops items. It does not make rarer cards.
