## Before you build

- Prestige Tower battles are **4v4**.
- Enemies get stronger every time you prestige. At floor 3,001 they are about 130–143x base strength.
- Source matters: pack, weather and boss cards beat general-pool cards of the same rarity. See [[Card Sources]].
