## Earning scrap

{{item ref="currency:scrap"}}

### Sacrificing duplicates

You keep **4 copies** of each card. Copies beyond that can be sacrificed for scrap. **Each border counts separately:**

- 5 Shiny Galactic copies → 1 to sacrifice.
- 4 plain Galactic copies → nothing to sacrifice.

### Recycling

You can also recycle items and materials at the [[place:recycler]]. The rate is poor, so it is not recommended.

:::tip
The [[gamepass:more-scrap]] pass gives +50% scrap from recycling.
:::

## Scrap Shop

{{panel ref="place:scrap-shop" heading=3}}

- **Levels cost scrap**, and there is a **daily XP cap**.
- Each level unlocks new stock.
- **Level 5** unlocks the [[pack:forge]].
- The [[place:recycler]] itself is unlocked through the Scrap Shop.
